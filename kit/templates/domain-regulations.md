# Regulations in scope

The decision on each regulation of the packs in `[domain] packs` (SPEC-0074 rules 7 and 9), one list line each, in
the form `<pack id>.<regulation id>: <in scope|out of scope>, <one plain reason>`. A person confirms each decision; the
kit supports compliance work and gives no legal advice. `domain_model.py regulation` writes them; `check_domains.py`
checks the form.
