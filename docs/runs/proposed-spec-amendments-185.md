# Proposed spec amendments 185 (SPEC-0039; REQ-046)

Date: 2026-11-19 (from `date -u`). Approved specs change only through a proposal like this one (principle 6). Written for
ADR-0647 (brief #1539), which stays `Proposed` for the maintainers' decision. The path:
`docs/specs/0039-the-conductor-merges-a-cleared-pull-request-through-one-guarded-command.md`. Brief #1539 reserved
amendment 185. This file proposes the lines and does not edit the spec; the owner approves the amendment.

The sentence (brief #1539): "The owner merges a held pull request from a phone with one comment naming its head, the kit
runs the owner's guarded merge on it, and a button merge is recorded and its ADRs raised, in the kit and in every
project."

## Why

Measured on the default branch at d6d21c02 (2026-11-19), the brief's figures reproduced (session rule 19): the squash
commits of #1522 (5bbf1618), #1525 (deda2133) and #1521 (ea420a7f) each carry 0 lines `ADR-nnnn Accepted (Final <id>)`
and 0 lines `Merged-by: merge_cleared.py`; the squash of #1537 (d6d21c02), merged through the guarded command, carries 2.
ADR-0616, ADR-0639 and ADR-0643 each read `- Status: Proposed`. Issue #1536 (open, a brief, not a pull request) names
ADR-0639 and ADR-0643 and not ADR-0616.

The owner's merge today is `merge_cleared.py --pr <n> --owner` (ADR-0570 decision 2), which needs a terminal outside an
agent session (`SESSION_MARKERS`). A phone has none, so the owner used the forge's merge button, which skips the would-be
merge, the record line, the `Final:` lines and the post-merge governance run. The gap is twofold: an owner-only hold
(`OWNER_HOLDS`: `release`, `protected class`) has no phone path, and a head whose local evidence the owner accepted has no
prompt merge until the conductor's next `merge-queue` pass, which runs only while a conductor session is awake.

## Items

1. **Rule 5b, the owner's word (new).** "A comment is the owner's word on pull request `<n>` when all of these hold, read
   by REST only:
   - its body, after removing blank lines, is exactly one line `Merge: #<n> <h>`, `<h>` the full 40-character lower-case
     sha, with nothing before or after on that line and nothing else in the comment; a line inside a quote (`>`), a
     `Maintainer answer:`, a `Tell:` or any relay never counts;
   - it is posted on pull request `<n>` itself, and counts only as `created`: an edited comment never counts;
   - its author is in `spec_approvers` and is a maintainer (a `teams.maintainers` user handle by equality, a team by one
     REST membership read; a membership that cannot be read refuses), is of type `User`, is not in `conductor_accounts`,
     and the comment was not posted through an app (`performed_via_github_app` empty);
   - the comment carries no `Who:` line and no generator footer (the line `_Generated by` of a session's post);
   - it is newer than the clearing verdict on `<h>` (rule 3 or rule 3a; in `solo`, the person's `Confirmed:`), so a line
     never stands for a verdict.
   A word stands for head `<h>` only, until the head moves, a verdict that does not clear `<h>` is newer, `agk:hold` is
   set, or a newer word names another head (Q-1539-2). The owner's word lifts the holds of `OWNER_HOLDS` for that head as
   `--owner` does; in a team it also asks for the merge now. It is also the accept of rule 4 of `<h>`'s local evidence
   (Q-1539-6, the owner's answer), so a phone merge is one comment, under the guards of rule 4's new sentence (item 4a)."
2. **Rule 2 gains the new form and its callers.** "A second form of the guarded command reads the owner's word of rule 5b
   on one pull request and, when it stands, runs the `--owner` path on its head: `python3 scripts/agk/merge_cleared.py
   --pr <n> --word <comment id>`; the build of (a) may name the option otherwise and its brief settles it (Q-1539-9). It
   runs `outcome` as `--owner` does, with the same holds in the same order: the pinned head (`--sha` is the word's `<h>`;
   a moved head is refused, naming the new head in 12 characters), the clearing verdict, the red branch (only the
   red-branch fix passes), the record issue, the local or CI evidence, every hold of `hold_list` but `OWNER_HOLDS`, the
   would-be merge of rule 6 with governance and `pre_merge_tests`, re-prove (`reprove_check`) and `max_behind_merges`.
   It merges by rule 6 and records by rule 7. Unlike `--owner`, it is not refused for `SESSION_MARKERS`: its trust is
   the person's word, not the terminal (rule 5b, Residual). Three callers run it, and whichever runs first merges; the
   `merge-cleared` lock and the pinned sha stop a second merge:
   - (A) hosted: the merger job on the `issue_comment` `created` event whose first line starts `Merge:` (its `if:` and
     `event_target`), merging with `AGK_MERGE_TOKEN` as the job does today;
   - (D) local: the supervisor's scripted pass (`conductor.py watchdog --pass`), on the machine that holds the plan
     lease, merging as the pass's signed-in account; a new comment already ends the pass's quiet read (`change_reads`),
     so this adds no poll;
   - (C) the conductor's `merge-queue`, which includes a head with a standing word in its batch.
   In both modes the comment id is the person's mark. With `[flow] merge_queue` on, the form queues the head as the `--sha`
   form does under rule 2a and does not merge it itself (Q-1539-4)."
3. **Rule 2 gains the reply.** "The form posts one comment on the pull request per word and outcome, first word `Merge
   done:`, `Merge held:` or `Merge refused:`, then the head in 12 characters and the reason by rule or setting, never a
   login or value, ending with the kit's `Who:` line (`write_once`). A second run with the same word and the same reason
   posts nothing. A comment that fails rule 5b's author tests (an account outside the lists, a bot, an app, a `Who:` line)
   gets no reply and no merge: one line on standard error only, so the comments of a public repository cannot make the
   kit write."
4. **Rule 5 gains one sentence.** "A release or a protected-class hold (`OWNER_HOLDS`) also ends for one head on the
   owner's word of rule 5b; every other hold of this list still holds, and the word never lifts a hold for another head."
4a. **Rule 4 gains one sentence.** "In local mode the owner's word of rule 5b is also the accept of the difference for
   the head it names, as a `Maintainer answer:` accept is, for that exact head only, only when its author is a
   maintainer (rule 5b), and only when every check of `required_checks` has a latest run on that head that ended
   `success` (its secret scan included); a required check that is red, pending or missing on that head leaves the
   evidence unaccepted and the pull request held. The would-be merge with the default branch of rule 6 still runs. The
   record line of rule 7 and the `Merge done:` reply say the word stood for the merge and the accept."
5. **Rule 6 gains one line of the squash message.** "A merge on the owner's word adds, after the `Final:` and spec lines,
   one line `Merge-word: <comment id>`, then `Merged-by: merge_cleared.py`. `holds_record` also holds a pull request whose
   body or any commit message already carries a `Merge-word:` line."
6. **Rule 7 gains the record of a hand merge.** "A merge into the default branch whose squash commit lacks `Merged-by:
   merge_cleared.py` is a hand merge. It is found, hosted, by `route.py` on the `pull_request` `closed` event it already
   handles (beside `finish_brief`); locally, by the supervisor's pass and at the conductor's start, by `git log
   --first-parent` of the fetched default branch since the last recorded merge, with no GitHub read when nothing new
   merged. For each one the kit posts one line `Hand merge: #<n> <merge sha> <UTC time> <login>` on the record issue of
   this rule (Q-1539-7), and, for each ADR the pull request added or changed that reads `Proposed` on the default branch,
   one `Owner needed:` comment on the merged pull request naming the ADR, whether a `Final:` line named the merged head,
   and the documents-only status fix that the conductor files through the `brief` skill (as #1536 does). Nothing is
   reverted, and nothing is accepted by the record itself. A merge made by GitHub's merge queue carries no record line in
   its message, so it is recorded the same way (Q-1539-4)."
7. **Rule 8 gains.** "The conductor's guard allows the `--word` form only as the conductor's `merge-queue` subprocess runs
   it, and keeps denying `--owner`. Every role guard and `.claude/hooks/data_guard.py`, which every session runs, builders
   included, deny posting a comment whose first line starts `Merge:`; `session.py tell` refuses it (`TELL_REFUSED`), and
   the steward never relays one. The command still never posts a `Verdict:`, `Approve:`, `Confirmed:`, `Maintainer
   answer:` or `Merge:` line; its replies of item 3 start `Merge done:`, `Merge held:` or `Merge refused:`."
8. **Rule 11, the guide.** "`docs/guides/auto-merge.md` gains how the owner merges from the browser or a phone: copy the
   `Merge: #<n> <head>` line that the conductor's `Owner needed:` text and its `not queued:` line (`kept_out_line`) print
   beside `OWNER_COMMAND` (for an owner-held pull request the text shows this one line, which also accepts the local
   evidence, item 4a); what each reply means; that the forge's button leaves a `Hand merge:` record and ADRs to raise;
   and that personal mode has no line. `docs/guides/local-mode.md` names the delay of one `[autopilot] interval_minutes`."

## Questions (brief #1539), each with the reading this proposal takes

- Q-1539-1: does principle 2 need new words? No; the merge is still `merge_cleared.py`, with `auto_merge = true`, and the
  lift rests on the person's own word; a change of `constitution/` is a separate change on the owner's word.
- Q-1539-2: does a held word stand for its head until the head moves? Yes, for that exact head only, ended by a moved
  head, a non-clearing verdict, `agk:hold` or a newer word; one reply per reason.
- Q-1539-3: does the word work with `auto_merge` off, as `--owner` does (Q-1358-1 (a))? No; the reply points to `--owner`
  at a terminal.
- Q-1539-4: with `merge_queue` on, may a word join the queue, whose message carries no record? Yes, as the guarded
  command does, with item 6 recording the merge and raising its ADRs; the `Final:` lines of a queued merge are a later
  brief.
- Q-1539-5: in a team, is one maintainer's word enough? Yes, as `--owner` takes one; a two-person setting is a later
  brief.
- Q-1539-6: does a `Merge:` word also stand for the local-evidence accept of the same head? Answered by the owner
  (quoted as data from brief #1539, 2026-11-19, the co-owner chat, 17:55 IST, option "a": "Yes, one comment does both"):
  yes, for the exact head only, from a maintainer, only with the forge's required checks green on that head (its secret
  scan included), the merge still tested against the default branch, and the record saying the word stood for both
  (item 4a).
- Q-1539-7: is the hand-merge record `Hand merge:` or rule 7's `Auto merge:` line? `Hand merge:`, so the record tells a
  guarded merge from a button merge.
- Q-1539-8: priority? 1.
- Q-1539-9: the option's name. The brief names none; this proposal writes `--word <comment id>` and build (a)'s brief
  settles it.
