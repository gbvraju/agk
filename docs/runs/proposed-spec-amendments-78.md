# Proposed spec amendments 78 (SPEC-0009, SPEC-0022, SPEC-0024; REQ-042)

Date: 2026-10-25 (from `date -u`). Approved specs change only through a proposal like this one (principle 6). Applied by ADR-0142. The paths: `docs/specs/0009-facts.md`, `docs/specs/0022-the-weekly-product-report-also-publishes-the-sections-it-com.md`, `docs/specs/0024-the-report-s-cost-totals-carry-the-number-of-the-week-s-merg.md`. The number 78 was reserved for build (c) of SPEC-0035 on #207 by the conductor.

1. **SPEC-0009, the shadow section of the facts.** The document gains the optional section `shadow`: `{issue, unpaired, unread, weeks, total}` with the number of the one open issue labelled `agk-shadow`, the shadow comments that match no verdict head, the merged pull requests whose comments could not be read, the counts of each ISO week of the merge and the counts in total over the window of the facts; the counts are `heads`, `right_conductor_only`, `right_shadow_only`, `right_both`, `wrong_conductor`, `wrong_shadow`, `unmarked_heads` and `unmarked_findings` (SPEC-0035 rule 4). With no open issue it is `{"issue": null}`; with two, with an unreadable copy and with a refused mark it is `unknown`. `facts.json` keeps schema number 1.
2. **SPEC-0022, the number of the schema and the field.** `report.json` takes schema number 7 and `kit/report-schema.json` requires it. The item `week-totals` gains `shadow` (SPEC-0035 rule 5): an object `{week, total, unpaired, unread}` whose `week` and `total` hold the eight counts and `catch_rate`, or `not-running`, or `unknown`.
3. **SPEC-0024, the catch rate beside the verdicts.** The markdown of the totals line prints the shadow review after the estimate: the heads reviewed by both, the right findings of the conductor only, of the shadow only and of both, the wrong findings of each, the unmarked heads and the catch rate of the week and of the window; `none` when no finding is right, never 0, and `shadow review: not running` or `unknown` otherwise.
