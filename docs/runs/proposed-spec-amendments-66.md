# Proposed spec amendments 66 (SPEC-0009, SPEC-0022, SPEC-0024; REQ-041, REQ-040, REQ-039)

Date: 2026-10-25 (from `date -u`). Approved specs change only through a proposal like this one (principle 6). Applied by ADR-0128. The paths: `docs/specs/0009-facts.md`, `docs/specs/0022-the-weekly-product-report-also-publishes-the-sections-it-com.md`, `docs/specs/0024-the-report-s-cost-totals-carry-the-number-of-the-week-s-merg.md`. The number 66 was reserved for this build on #176 by the conductor. It builds rule 10 of SPEC-0034, rule 5 (the last sentence) of SPEC-0033 and rule 11 of SPEC-0032 as one change of the report, so that `report.json` changes its schema once.

1. **SPEC-0009, the facts of a pull request.** The pull-request record carries the optional fields `brief` (the `n` of its line `Brief: #n`, `null` without one), `flow` (the hours of its four steps, `taken_to_pull_request`, `to_first_verdict`, `verdict_to_cleared` and `cleared_to_merge`, each a decimal string with one digit, `null` for a step that did not happen and `unknown` for one that cannot be read), `conflicts` (its comments whose first line starts `conflict needs a person:`), `renumbers` (its commits whose first line names a renumber), `corrections` (the moments of the `Correction:` comments of listed verdict authors) and `stalled` (SPEC-0033 rule 5). The document gains the section `flow`: `max_builders` and `stall_hours` of the `[flow]` table of `agk.toml` (3 and 4 by default, `unknown` for an invalid value), `briefs` (the open `agk-brief` issues with their number, title, creation moment and what they advance), `retakes` (the moments of the `taken:` comments that say `retakes`) and `corrections` (the moments of all `Correction:` comments of listed authors on pull requests and briefs). Each of the last three is `unknown` when a saved copy it needs cannot be read. `facts.json` keeps schema number 1.
2. **SPEC-0022, the Flow section.** The report gains a sixth section after Cost, `Flow` (`flow` in `report.json`), with one item `flow-figures`: the open builder pull requests against `max_builders`, the median hours of each of the four steps over the pull requests merged in the week (`none` when none was measured, `unknown` when one cannot be read), and the week's conflicts, renumbers and retakes. At risk gains three kinds: `pull-requests-stalled` (SPEC-0033 rule 5), `briefs-idle` (an open brief older than 7 days that no pull request names) and `requirements-without-brief` (a requirement not `done` that no open brief names); what cannot be read is one `unknown` item. `report.json` takes schema number 6 and `kit/report-schema.json` requires it.
3. **SPEC-0024, the corrections beside the verdicts.** The item `week-totals` gains `corrections`, the number of the conductor's `Correction:` comments of the week (SPEC-0032 rule 11), or `unknown`; the markdown prints `corrections: N` right after `fix verdicts in total`.
