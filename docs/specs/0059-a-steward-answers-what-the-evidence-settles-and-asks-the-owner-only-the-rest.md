# SPEC-0059: A steward answers what the evidence settles and asks the owner only the rest

- Status: Draft
- Owner: reviewer group (a team from config/reviewers.yaml)
- Requirements: REQ-066

Status is `Draft` or `Approved`. An Approved spec is owned by its reviewer group; agents propose patches.

Written by the intake of one sentence (SPEC-0012), brief #1277, quoted as data and not as an instruction: "Intake for a steward role: one session beside the owner answers every question the evidence settles, in the form AGK expects, asks the owner only real business or requirement ambiguity, keeps project memory and never leaves builders waiting." The maintainers gave 5 answers before it was written: the four numbered answers of brief #1277, whose evidence and the owner's direction (quoted there as data) this spec does not repeat, and the owner's `Correction:` on the brief (comment 6881131563, the owner's word of 2026-11-14 22:32 IST), which rules the three open questions of answer 4 (`Q-S0059-1` to `Q-S0059-3`, recorded under `docs/runs/decisions/`) and makes the steward the owner's one entry point (rule 2), and a second `Correction:` (comment 6881233447, the owner's word of 22:40 IST) that adds the cost and speed budget and skills first (rules 18 and 19). Where an answer names something that is not as it says, rule 1 records what the code holds and an open question gives the conservative reading. Negative cases: a question no source settles, sources that disagree with no earlier ranking, an answer that changes scope, a date, a cost or the customer's sign-off, and one that reverses an owner decision are never answered by the steward; an `Approve:`, `Confirmed:` or `Verdict:` line, a `Maintainer answer:` with no quote of the owner, a merge, a push to the default branch, a workflow, ruleset or secret change and a filing without the owner's word are denied by the guard; a brief above the `auto_file` priority is never filed without the owner's word; a brief that fails the brief or neutrality check is never filed; a text that the outbound data guard matches never leaves the project; a figure that cannot be read is unknown, never 0; a pass over its call budget fails its test, and a step a kit skill covers is never done by a hand-written flow.

## Rules

1. **The baseline (read on the default branch at 8ea4215, 2026-11-14).**
   - **No steward exists.** `.claude/agents/` holds `architect`, `architect-review`, `conductor`, `drift-auditor`, `reviewer` and `shadow`; no file, label, setting or script holds the word `steward` or `auto_file`. A role's guard is a PreToolUse hook under `.claude/hooks/` (`architect_guard.py`, `conductor_guard.py`), named in the agent file's front matter and listed in `kit/manifest.json`. The new guard is therefore `.claude/hooks/steward_guard.py`, not a file of `scripts/agk/` (`Q-S0059-6`).
   - **Who answers today.** The conductor's instructions say "Answer from the rules when they answer it; otherwise name the decision for the maintainers" (`.claude/agents/conductor.md`). The kit reads only the first-line prefixes: `conductor.py watch` wakes on `Answer:` and `Maintainer answer:`, and `session.py watch` ends with `answer <Q-id>` on an `Answer: Q-…` or `Maintainer answer: Q-…` by an answerer that names a Q id of the author's `Question:`. **The form `Answer: Q-<id> <answer> (evidence: …; rule: …)` is not written anywhere in the kit**; the brief calls it the form the conductor already posts. Rule 4 defines it, keeping the prefix that the readers match.
   - **Who may decide.** The brief's answer 2 names principle 1 as "who may answer a question". Principle 1 is the ADR rule for a protected change. The rules on questions are principle 7 ("Questions are recorded, never guessed") and `CLAUDE.md item 1` in `config/core-rules.md` ("You implement; you never decide … record it as a `Q-…` question"). Rule 15 changes those, not principle 1 (`Q-S0059-5`).
   - **One login.** `config/reviewers.yaml` lists one `spec_approvers` login, and the sessions of this repository post as that same login (the `Started:` of this brief is by it). `route.py` treats a `Maintainer answer:` by a spec approver as the owner's answer and clears `Owner needed:` with it. So the login cannot show that an owner's answer came from the owner: only the quote of rule 5 and the guard of rule 11 can (`Q-S0059-4`).
   - **What exists to build on.** `agk:parked` is a person's hold that the router never sets or removes (`route.py`), and `session.py next --take` skips a parked pull request; `session.py after` sends a finished builder to its next brief or to the conductor; `check_brief.py` labels a malformed brief `agk-brief-invalid`, and `session.py plan` prints its `MALFORMED:` line; `check_neutrality.py` checks a text against `config/neutrality.txt`; `docs/sources/` is the folder of a project's source documents (SPEC-0043); the signed-off prototype is SPEC-0037's frozen version; ADR-0430's fresh reviewer reads a bundle only; SPEC-0045 names the `strongest` tier; `session.py tell` posts a `Tell:` on the `agk-conductor` issue.
2. **The role.** `.claude/agents/steward.md`, started as `claude --agent steward` `(verify: the flag of the runtime used)`. One steward per project. Its kind is `design` (SPEC-0045 rule 2): `strongest` at `high`. It starts on demand only: by the owner, or by the conductor through the launch adapter (`launch.py`, as builders are started) when a `Question:` has waited one conductor pass with no `Answer:`, `Maintainer answer:` or `Correction:` after it (`Q-S0059-3`, ruled). It runs no watch loop, no `/loop` and no lease renew loop: it reads, answers, records and ends when its queue is empty (no `Question:` it can settle or relay, no finding to triage, no owner reply to post). A start by the conductor that the runtime refuses is listed as stuck, never silent, as SPEC-0058 rule 12 holds for a session's start of another (`Q-S0059-11`). A start posts `Started: steward <session> <UTC time>` on the memory issue; a second start that finds one newer than 60 minutes with no `Ended: steward <session>` after it says so and writes nothing (`Q-S0059-7`).
   - **The owner's one entry point.** The owner opens the steward and asks; the owner works through it. On start it reads the repository and its memory and names the project's stage on SPEC-0048's guided path, in the owner's words: start, vision and discovery, scope, prototype and design, stack and technical design, plan, build (`Q-S0059-10` maps them to SPEC-0048 rule 1's phases). It drives that stage with the kit's existing skills (`start`, `vision`, `scope-review`, `design`, `stack`, `plan-intake`, `scope-lock`), asking the owner only what rule 3 cannot settle, and it re-implements none of them: each skill's own rules, gates and the owner's go of SPEC-0048 rule 9 stay as they are, and the steward never posts a go.
   - **Handing to the conductor.** When the plan passes its checks (the trace of `check_plan.py`, no `MALFORMED:` line of `session.py plan`, the scope locked by `scope-lock`, and no open owner question that blocks the first milestone), the steward starts the conductor with `launch.py` `(verify: the adapter's start of a conductor)`, and from then on works beside it: answers, triage and memory. The conductor starts the builds, as today.
3. **The evidence rule.** A question is settled by evidence when one source answers it. The sources are read in this order, and the first that answers wins:
   1. the owner's earlier decisions: `Maintainer answer:` lines, Accepted ADRs and the steward memory's decisions (rule 8);
   2. Approved specs and `docs/requirements.md`;
   3. the signed-off prototype (SPEC-0037);
   4. the source documents under `docs/sources/`;
   5. AGK's rules (`constitution/principles.md`, `config/core-rules.md`, `config/session-rules.md`, the agent files) and the kit's code.
   A question goes to the owner (rule 5), never answered by the steward, when no source answers it; when two sources answer it differently and no earlier decision ranks them; when the answer changes scope, a date, a cost or what the customer signs off; or when it reverses an earlier owner decision. A question about the kit itself (a make target, a setting, a label, a conductor or router behaviour) is settled by step 5 when the code answers it, and the steward reproduces the behaviour on the default branch before it answers (rule 9).
4. **The answer.** One comment, on the pull request or brief that holds the `Question:`, whose first line is `Answer: Q-<id> <answer> (evidence: <source and place>; rule: <rule>)`. The place is a file and its rule or line, a comment id, or a commit, as read in this pass. A requirement answer (one settled by step 2, 3 or 4 of rule 3) is posted only after rule 13's re-check agrees. The steward posts no `Answer:` on a question that rule 3 sends to the owner.
5. **The owner's question.** One plain business sentence with the choices and the steward's recommendation, with no kit term (no file name, label, rule number or command): for example "Should the report go out on Friday as planned, or wait a week for the second data source? I recommend waiting: the first source alone misses two of the five totals." It is recorded as one memory comment (rule 8) and given to the owner where the owner is: the chat in which the steward runs, or, in personal mode, printed. On the pull request or brief the steward posts `Question: Q-<id> for the maintainers: <the same sentence>`, so the owner's label filter finds it (brief #1043). The owner's reply is posted as one comment whose first line is `Maintainer answer: Q-<id> <answer>` and whose second line is `Owner said (<UTC time>): "<the owner's words, verbatim>"`. The steward never paraphrases inside the quote, never posts a `Maintainer answer:` without that line and never posts one for a reply it did not receive in this pass.
6. **Triage.** Every finding becomes exactly one of:
   - **a kit brief, correction or question**, filed straight to the kit repository, from the kit itself or from a customer project (`Q-S0059-1`, ruled: the kit stores nothing of a project), with invented names only (`example.com`), after `check_brief.py` passes on a brief's text and the outbound data guard below passes on every text;
   - **a project brief**, filed only after `check_brief.py` passes on its text, so that no `MALFORMED:` line and no `agk-brief-invalid` label follows;
   - **an owner question** (rule 5).
   The steward's memory records which one and why (rule 8). Filing needs the owner's word for that brief, a line the owner wrote in this pass naming it, unless its `Priority:` is at or below `[steward] auto_file` (rule 12; `0` by default, `Q-S0059-2`, ruled) and `check_brief.py` passes and, for the kit repository, the outbound data guard passes. Each brief filed without the owner's word is listed in the daily list (rule 13).
   - **The outbound data guard.** Before any text leaves the project for the kit repository (an issue, a comment, a correction), it is checked, fail closed, against: the project's own `config/neutrality.txt` tokens; the kit's neutrality list; and the detectors of `guard.scan_text` (SPEC-0042 rule 10) for e-mail addresses and phone numbers, plus URLs of the project's hosts (the hosts of its git remotes and its `[guard] patterns`, `Q-S0059-12`) and repository names other than the kit's. A match refuses the post with one line naming the setting and the class, never the value, in SPEC-0042's refusal form, and the steward rewrites the text with invented names. A text that cannot be checked (a list that cannot be read) is refused, never posted. It relates to the two existing checks so: SPEC-0042's layer 1 (`data_guard.py`) judges what a session reads and writes in its own tool calls, and `check_neutrality.py` judges the tracked files of a repository; neither judges a text posted to another repository, which is this guard's one job. It reuses `guard.scan_text` and the neutrality list's reading and holds no second detector or token list. It is build (e).
7. **Maturity rules.** The steward carries at least the co-owner's level, and each rule is one the brief's evidence shows was missed:
   - nothing is stated as current that was not read in this pass;
   - before every brief or `Correction:` it checks the effect in GitHub mode, local mode and personal mode, and writes one line `Modes: github <effect>; local <effect>; personal <effect>` in it;
   - one dependency per `Depends-on:` line;
   - a pull request is called complete only after it is compared with every `Correction:` on its brief, and one posted after its build started is named;
   - a cause is reproduced on the default branch before it is filed;
   - when it was wrong, it says so in one line where it was wrong and records the lesson (rule 8);
   - an unconfirmed fact is marked `(verify)`.
8. **Memory.** One open issue labelled `agk-steward`, created by the steward if none is open. Each decision, open owner question, triage choice and lesson is one comment with its evidence, its first line one of `Decision:`, `Owner question:`, `Triage:` or `Lesson:`. A start reads the issue first, newest last, before any other step. At 500 comments the steward opens a new issue labelled `agk-steward` whose first comment links the old one and carries forward every open owner question and the override share of rule 13, then closes the old one. No customer data, secret or personal value is written to it; the data guard holds for each comment (SPEC-0042).
9. **Reading and reproducing.** The steward reads GitHub with GET only, as session rule 10 and `CLAUDE.md item 10` hold, and the default branch after `git fetch` (session rule 33), and stops when the project is paused. A behaviour it answers or files is run on the default branch where a command can show it, and the command and its verbatim output are its evidence.
10. **Never blocking.** A pull request whose question goes to the owner is labelled `agk:parked` by the steward. Its builder posts `Report: <head> handing over: blocked: <Q-id> waits for the owner` and ends with `session.py after`, and the conductor fills the slot with the next brief, as it does for any parked work. On the owner's answer the steward posts the `Maintainer answer:` of rule 5, removes `agk:parked`, and tells the conductor with `session.py tell` (`Tell: <conductor> parked #<n> answered Q-<id>`). The conductor starts a builder on the same branch and pull request (`session.py resume --pr <n>`, session rule 9), never a second pull request. One open pull request per session stands. In strict `pr_mode` a parked pull request is still the person's one open pull request, so parking frees no slot there (`Q-S0059-8`).
11. **Limits, guarded.** `steward_guard.py`, the role's PreToolUse hook, denies:
    - a comment whose first line starts `Approve:`, `Confirmed:`, `Verdict:`, `Final:` or `Correction:`;
    - a comment whose first line starts `Maintainer answer:` and whose second line is not of rule 5's `Owner said (<UTC time>): "…"` form;
    - a merge, `merge_cleared.py`, auto-merge, a push to the default branch, a force push, a tag;
    - a change to a workflow file (`.github/`), the ruleset, a required check or a secret;
    - an issue creation (a brief) whose `Priority:` is above `[steward] auto_file`, unless the command carries the owner's word line of rule 6 `(verify: the hook can read it from the command)`;
    - a post to the kit repository from a project whose text the outbound data guard of rule 6 matches or cannot check;
    - any label write other than `agk:parked` on a pull request and `agk-steward` on its memory issue.
    Each denial names the rule and the setting or path class, never the value (principle 8). The guard reads no network and stores nothing. The guard checks the form of the quote; it cannot check that the owner said it (rule 1), which is why the quote carries the owner's time and words for the trail.
12. **The setting.** An optional `[steward]` table of `agk.toml`: `auto_file`, the highest brief priority the steward files without the owner's word, an integer from 0 to 3, or `false` for none (default `0`: only a `Priority: 0` brief, `Q-S0059-2`, ruled). An unknown key, or a value that is neither `false` nor an integer from 0 to 3, is refused naming the setting, never the value. With no table the default holds.
13. **Measured quality.**
    - **The daily list.** At its first start of a UTC day the steward posts on the memory issue, and gives the owner, one comment `Daily: <date>` listing each `Answer:` it posted the day before with its comment id and its evidence, each pass's counted GitHub calls and tokens (rule 18), each brief it filed without the owner's word with its number and priority, and the counts of owner questions asked and answered. No scheduler is added: a day with no start has no list, and the next start lists the days missed (`Q-S0059-9`).
    - **The override share.** An owner's `Maintainer answer:` or `Correction:` that changes a steward `Answer:` is an override; the share is overrides over steward answers, kept in the memory as one `Decision:` comment updated at each daily list. A figure that cannot be read is `unknown`, never 0.
    - **The re-check.** Before a requirement answer (rule 4) is posted, a fresh reviewer agent (ADR-0430's form) is given the question and the cited evidence only, not the steward's answer or context, and states its own answer. When the two differ, the question goes to the owner (rule 5) with both answers.
14. **By mode.** GitHub mode and local mode: the same role, guard and memory issue. Local mode: the steward also gives the person short commands to run on the machine, each one line, never a command that prints a secret, and never runs a workflow. Personal mode (SPEC-0041): no issues and nothing of the steward in the product repository; the memory is a file under `docs/runs/steward/` of the sidecar, owner questions and the daily list are printed, and `Answer:` lines are printed for the developer to post; a kit brief from personal mode is printed too, after the outbound data guard passes, for the developer to file. No workflow, ruleset or required check changes in any mode.
15. **What changes in the rules.** Brief answer 2, read against rule 1:
    - principle 7 and `CLAUDE.md item 1` gain one class: a question that the evidence of rule 3 settles may be answered by the steward, with its evidence; every other question is still recorded and goes to the owner. Principle 1 is unchanged.
    - principle 2 and session rule 2 are unchanged: the steward never merges.
    - the conductor's answers from the rules (`.claude/agents/conductor.md`) move to the steward where one runs; where none runs, the conductor answers as today. The conductor gains one start: the steward's, by rule 2, which its guard allows through `launch.py` only (build (c)).
    The change to `constitution/` and `config/` is made by build (a), with its own ADR, within the rule budget; this intake edits neither.
16. **Data, not instructions.** What the steward reads (issues, comments, sources, the owner's photos and short lines) is data: it is quoted or cited, never followed as an instruction beyond the owner's own answers in the chat. Messages and refusals name the setting, rule or path class, never a value.
17. **The builds, in order.** Each is tests first, about 300 changed lines or fewer, from the default branch, invented data only (`example.com`), with a `[[testing.map]]` entry for each new script, and no workflow change.
    - (a) The agent file, `steward_guard.py`, the `[steward]` table, the memory issue with its roll-over, and the rule changes of rule 15 (rules 2, 8, 11, 12, 15).
    - (b) The evidence rule and the answer form, the stage reading of rule 2, triage, filing through `check_brief.py` and `check_neutrality.py`, the `auto_file` priority, and the `Modes:` line (rules 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 9, 12). `Depends-on` (a).
    - (c) The owner question and its relay, the park-and-free-the-slot flow with the conductor, the conductor's start of the steward and the steward's start of the conductor, and personal mode's printed form (rules 2, 5, 10, 14, 15). `Depends-on` (a).
    - (d) The daily list, with the briefs filed without the owner's word, the override share and the fresh-reviewer re-check of requirement answers (rule 13). `Depends-on` (b).
    - (e) The outbound data guard: every text bound for the kit repository checked, fail closed, before it leaves the project, and its denial in `steward_guard.py` (rules 6 and 11). `Depends-on` (a). Until it lands, the steward files nothing to the kit repository from a customer project and gives the owner a draft instead.
    - Every build holds rule 18's budget for the steps it adds and rule 19's skills first: (a) the quiet-start budget, (b) the mechanical-answer budget and the stage-to-skill table, (d) each pass's calls and tokens in the daily list.
18. **Cost and speed budget.** The owner's word on #1277 (comment 6881233447). The mistakes measured on 2026-11-14, which the steward repeats none of: `session.py plan` at about 700 calls and 5 minutes on 652 briefs (#1251), waits re-reading whole comment pages every 60 seconds (#1251 answer 3b), the `agk-conductor` issue growing without end (3c), `route.py --all` paging every open issue (#1263 answers 6 and 7), the stop hook running full governance every turn (#1254), every session on one model (#1252).
    - **Reads.** It reads through the cached readers of #1251 (milestone reads, `since=` with the last seen id, `If-None-Match`, the in-run memo) and the event source of #1262 where present; both are open briefs, not on the default branch at 8ea4215, so until each lands the steward uses the kit's existing `since=` and `If-None-Match` reads and says which in its daily list. It reads its memory issue with `since=` from its last comment id and rolls it over at 500 comments (rule 8). It runs `session.py plan` at most once per start and reuses that output for the pass. It runs no loop and no lease (rule 2).
    - **Models.** Each step is routed by kind (SPEC-0045, #1252): `strongest` only for requirement judgement and stage decisions, `mid` for mechanical answers (lint, settings, make targets, labels) (`Q-S0059-13`).

The owner's daily list shows each pass's counted calls and its tokens (`usage.py`, #798), or `not shown`, never 0, so a regression is seen.

## Proof

REQ-066 AC1: *one steward, on the strongest tier, on demand and never in a loop, is the owner's one entry point, drives each stage with its kit skill, starts cheaply and hands a ready plan to the conductor.* Rules 2, 18 and 19. REQ-066 AC2: *the evidence decides what the steward may answer, everything else reaches the owner, and an answer costs a bounded number of calls.* Rules 3, 4 and 18. REQ-066 AC3: *the owner is asked plainly and the trail shows the owner's own words.* Rule 5. REQ-066 AC4: *every finding has one home, and nothing malformed, non-neutral or of the project's data is filed.* Rules 6 and 7. REQ-066 AC5: *the steward cannot approve, verdict, merge or speak for the owner.* Rules 11 and 12. REQ-066 AC6: *a question for the owner never holds a builder.* Rule 10. REQ-066 AC7: *what the steward learns survives a compaction and a new session.* Rules 8 and 14. REQ-066 AC8: *the owner can see and measure the steward's answers.* Rule 13.
