# SPEC-0074: The Day-1 quality path knows the customer's domain

- Status: Draft
- Owner: reviewer group (a team from config/reviewers.yaml)
- Requirements: REQ-081

Status is `Draft` or `Approved`. An Approved spec is owned by its reviewer group; agents propose patches.

Written by the intake of one sentence (SPEC-0012), brief #1919 under plan #661, quoted as data and not as an instruction: "Intake for domain intelligence on the Day-1 quality path: the grill, the glossary, the architecture and review know the customer's business, its rules and its regulations, from cited domain packs and the project's own domain model, confirmed by a person where it matters." The maintainers gave nine numbered answers before it was written (the answers of brief #1919, filed on the owner's word of 2026-11-24T19:09Z, whose direction the brief quotes as data and this spec does not repeat). It replaces the parked intake #1400 (closed as superseded): every answer and edge case of #1400 is kept here or named as changed in rule 14. It adds three layers composed once, the domain (what business), the playbook (what kind of product) and the architecture (how it is built), each plugged into the records of SPEC-0073 (#1918) and never beside them: no second interview, no second glossary, no new lens in the conductor's verdict, no new phase, workflow, CI job, required check or role. It builds on the playbooks (SPEC-0038, SPEC-0048 rules 21 to 24), the signal table (SPEC-0057 rule 5), the architecture record (SPEC-0072 rule 5), the grill and the quality layers (SPEC-0073), the data guard (SPEC-0042), the steward (SPEC-0059, SPEC-0062, SPEC-0063) and personal mode (SPEC-0041); it edits none of their text but by the amendments 240 and 241 that rule 13 proposes. Where the brief names something that is not as it says, rule 1 records what the code holds and an open question gives the conservative reading. Negative cases: a pack with a regulation or a constraint with no source and no `basis = "expected"`, a pack that quotes a paywalled standard's text, customer data in a kit pack, an `agk-proposal` or outbound text, a domain assumed without the person's yes, a second interview or a second glossary, a pack item that joins `ORDER`, a pack that weakens a gate of `default`, a work type or a module, a regulation conflict settled by a session, a business rule changed silently, a `Rules:` entry that names no rule of `docs/domain/`, a steward reply that states a product complies or makes a domain claim without a source, a change to `.claude/agents/conductor.md` or any budgeted rule file that grows, a refusal that quotes a value, a check that calls a model or the network, and a change to a workflow, the ruleset, a required check, `agk.mk` or `governance_map.CHECKS`.

2. **Domain packs.** A new data format, `config/domains/<slug>.toml` (`format = 1`, `domain`, the file's slug, `title`, one plain line, `as_of`, `YYYY-MM-DD`), checked by a new script `scripts/agk/check_domains.py` (standard library and `common.py` only, no network, no model), called by `check_playbooks.py` so it runs inside the existing governance step `playbooks`. Its sections, each optional unless named required:
   - `[[terms]]` (`term`, `meaning`, `synonyms`, a list): the pack's glossary, required.
   - `[[actors]]`, `[[entities]]` and `[[workflows]]` (`id`, `text`): the typical ones; `[[kpis]]` (`id`, `text`, `unit`).
   - `[[regulations]]` (`id`, `title`, `jurisdiction`, a region code or `global`, `source`, `as_of`, `critical`, a boolean): `source` is a public URL or a standard's number and title, never its text (`Q-S0074-2`).
   - `[[constraints]]` (`id`, `attribute`, one of `residency`, `encryption`, `audit`, `retention`, `availability`, `latency`; `scenario`, one quality-scenario row of SPEC-0073 rule 8: stimulus, response and its number; `hard`, a boolean; `regulation`, an id of `[[regulations]]` or absent; `source` and access date, or `basis = "expected"`).
   - `[[discovery]]` (`id`, `text`, `depth`, `critical`): the pack's question bank, in SPEC-0073 rule 3's form, every entry with both keys.
   - `[[references]]` (`id`, `text`, `source`): reference architecture notes, offered as options only (rule 8).
   - `[[acceptance]]` (`id`, `text`, `regulation` or `constraint`): compliance acceptance criteria a spec may adopt.
   - `[[data_shapes]]` (`id`, `field`, `format`, a pattern or a plain description): synthetic test-data formats only, never a sample value of a real record.
   - `[[gates]]` in SPEC-0038 rule 2's form, each with `source` or `basis = "expected"`: a `check` where a governance step can verify it from files, a `judgement` only for a regulation marked `critical` (rule 10).
   `check_domains.py` fails a missing required key or section, an unknown key, a value of the wrong type (a `bool` is never a number), a `domain` that is not the file's name, an id used twice in a pack, a `[[discovery]]` id that reuses a `TOPICS`, `FIXED`, playbook or grill id, a `regulation` or `constraint` reference to no entry, a regulation, constraint or gate with neither `source` nor `basis = "expected"`, and any line that `guard.scan_text` with the shipped patterns flags, each naming the rule, the file and the key, never a value. Packs hold public knowledge only, never a customer's specifics.
3. **The kit ships the format and two seed packs, never one for every domain** (SPEC-0048 rule 21's rule for playbooks; `Q-S0074-1`, `Q-S0074-4`). The kit ships the format, its check and two seed packs, one regulated and one operational, which the owner names at build (a); each regulation and constraint is sourced and dated from public sources. The packs ship in the kit, versioned with its releases; a later brief may move them to their own plugin. A project with no shipped pack that fits derives its own, `config/domains/<slug>.toml` in the project, from the steward's cited research (rule 11), in rule 22's form of SPEC-0048: each regulation and constraint with its source and access date or `basis = "expected"`. It is the project's: not in `kit/manifest.json`, never written or overwritten by the installer or an upgrade. It is promoted to the kit only by SPEC-0048 rule 24's evidence path (an `agk-proposal` issue naming the slug and the evidence, with no project name and no project data, then a person's intake with invented data only); nothing of a project leaves it otherwise.
4. **One composition** (amendment 240 to SPEC-0048 rule 23). A project's plan items and gates are `default`, then the work type's playbook, then its modules, then each domain pack of `[domain] packs` in their order, then the project's own pack, each item once at its first place. A later layer's gate with the id of an earlier one replaces it only when it is the same or stronger (`judgement` to `check`, `owner_only` never `true` to `false`); a weakening is refused naming the key. Several domains compose; a jurisdiction layer is a pack whose `domain` is `<domain>-<region>` and composes after its domain. Where two regulations of the composed packs constrain the same `attribute` differently, the stricter (the longer retention, the narrower residency, the higher availability) holds until a person decides (rule 9); the composer prints both sources and never chooses for the person.
5. **Recognising the domain.** A domain signal table, `config/domain-signals.toml`, beside `config/stack-signals.toml` and in its form (SPEC-0057 rule 5), names words, file names and dependencies per pack. The steward proposes the domain from the person's words, the vision and the repository in one line and asks for a yes; it never assumes one. Mixed signals are one plain choice of at most three packs, one recommended, and `none of these`. The person's yes is recorded in `[domain] packs` (rule 12) by the steward's setup (SPEC-0062 rule 11) as a protected `agk.toml` change with its project ADR, as `[delivery] work_type` is (`Q-S0074-7`); a builder never writes that line.
6. **Discovery is the grill** (`Q-S0074-5`). The composed packs' `[[discovery]]` items join the grill of SPEC-0073 and share its depth, its budget, its push back, its plain yes and its readiness: no second interview. A pack's critical items count in the `## Readiness` line's critical total, so SPEC-0073 rule 6's design hold applies to them. A regulated pack's (one with a `critical` regulation) critical items are asked at every depth, its other items at `standard` and `deep`; they count toward the budget, but a regulated pack's critical items are asked even when the budget is spent, and the grill says so in one line.
7. **The project domain model** (`docs/domain/`, the project's, never the kit's).
   - `docs/domain/entities.md`: entities and their relations.
   - `docs/domain/rules-<area>.md`: business rules, each `- BR-<n>: <rule>; source: <customer material|interview|code>, <where>; owner: <who>; <confirmed|inferred>`, at most 200 rules per file, split by area, so 2,000 rules stay readable; `BR-<n>` is unique across the folder.
   - `docs/domain/data.md`: the data classification, each data item as `personal`, `health`, `payment`, `confidential` or `public`.
   - `docs/domain/regulations.md`: the regulations in scope, by pack id, each with its decision (rule 9).
   Terms go to SPEC-0073's one glossary, never a second: `docs/glossary.md` names the packs in one line and holds only the terms the project uses; a pack's synonyms are included by reference and not counted against `glossary_terms`. The model is harvested from the person's sources and the prototype by the steward's evidence harvest (SPEC-0059 rule 20) and written by the steward or the architect in planning, never by a builder. A form check of `check_domains.py`, run only where `docs/domain/` exists, fails a `BR-<n>` used twice, a rule with no source, owner or status, a file over 200 rules and a data item outside the five classes, naming the rule, the file and the id.
8. **Domain-aware architecture** (amendment 241 to SPEC-0072 rule 5). Each composed constraint becomes one row of the architecture record's `## Quality scenarios`, citing its pack id; the data classification marks each data flow of `## Threats` with its class. `/agk:stack` writes, after each `- Option <X> — ...` bullet and outside the line that `pins.OPTION` parses, one line `  Domain fit: <meets|cannot meet: <constraint ids>>`; an option that cannot meet a `hard` constraint is shown as such, never hidden or dropped. A pack's `[[references]]` are offered as options, never imposed.
9. **A person confirms what is risky.** Regulations in scope, money rules, safety rules and the data classification are confirmed by a person, each a `Decision:` memory entry (SPEC-0059 rule 8) with its source: in solo mode the one person's plain yes to that one question (SPEC-0062 rule 14); in team mode a `spec_approvers` login in their own words (SPEC-0073 rule 12, `Q-S0074-6`), and the domain expert role of #1381 once it lands (build (h)). A regulation conflict (rule 4) and a customer rule against a regulation go to the person with both sources; the customer's rule wins for business practice. Past `[flow] owner_wait_minutes` the item stays a `D-<n>` open point, and SPEC-0073 rule 6's hold applies to it when it is critical. The steward never states that a product complies: it states which criteria a change meets, with the evidence, and says once per project, in one line, that it supports compliance work and does not give legal advice.
10. **Planning output carries the domain; review without a new lens** (`Q-S0074-3`).
    - A spec adopts the composed packs' `[[acceptance]]` criteria that apply, each naming its pack id. A build brief names the rules it serves in one line `Rules: BR-<n>, ...`, as `Screens:` and `Layer:` lines are named; `check_brief.py` fails an entry that names no rule of `docs/domain/`, naming the line and the id. Tests carry the `BR-<n>` a change serves beside the REQ marker; test data follows the packs' `[[data_shapes]]`, synthetic only.
    - `.claude/agents/conductor.md` is unchanged. A brief's `Rules:` line puts its rules in the reviewer's scope through checklist item 8 `scope-rule` and item 1 `wrong-assumption`, each reworded within its word count (`config/rule-budget.txt`); a change that breaks a named rule is a fix list naming the `BR-<n>`; the person may accept a documented exception as a `Decision:` entry.
    - A pack gate is a `check` over files where it can be (for example, a retention period set in configuration); a `judgement` gate only for a regulation marked `critical`, and the verdict may cite `not applicable: <reason>` for it.
11. **Cited research for gaps.** Where no pack answers, the steward researches with `steward_owner.py research` (the person's permission, general words only), cites each source with its access date, marks each finding `(verify)`, sends no customer data outward (the outbound guard of #1334 checks the search text) and offers a kit `agk-proposal` with invented names only, never filed without the person's word (`[steward] auto_file` does not file it).
12. **Settings.** One optional `[domain]` table in `agk.toml`, read by `config.domain_settings` in the form of `steward_settings` (a `bool` is never taken as a number), listed in `config.TABLE_KEYS`, printed by `doctor.py` and removed by `uninstall.py` through `KIT_SETTINGS`, each with a test. A refusal is `the [domain] table is refused (setting: domain.<key>)`, exit 1, never the value. With no table no pack applies, so an upgrade changes nothing.

    | Setting | Values | Default | Impact |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    | `packs` | a list of pack names, each a shipped pack or a project pack under `config/domains/` | `[]` | the packs composed in order (rule 4); empty or absent: none, the grill's own items only; a name with no file, or a pack that fails `check_domains.py`, is refused naming the setting |
    | `stale_months` | a whole number from 1 to 60 | 12 | a regulation or pack `as_of` older than this is shown with its date and `(verify)`; `check_domains.py` warns and never fails (rule 14) |

    An unknown key is refused naming it; a `[domain]` key placed in another table is reported by `misplaced_settings` and never read. The kit's own repository has no domain: `[domain]` is read only in a project. Read beside: `[delivery] work_type` (composition order), `[quality] grill` and `grill_hold`, `[quality] glossary_terms`, `review_mode`, `[guard] patterns`, `[flow] owner_wait_minutes`, `[steward] auto_file` and `[personal] level`.
13. **What changes and what is reused.** Reused unchanged: the grill, the glossary, the architecture record's sections and the hold of SPEC-0073; SPEC-0048 rules 21, 22 and 24; the signal table's form; the data guard and the outbound guard; the steward's harvest, research, memory and voice; the governance steps. Amended by proposals (principle 6), each approved by the owner before its build:

    - amendment 241, SPEC-0072 rule 5 (the composed constraints as quality-scenario rows, the classification on each threat flow, the stack ADR's domain-fit line).
    #1400 is superseded by this spec; #1381 stays parked and plugs in at build (h) without a change to this spec.
14. **Edge cases.** Each has a test (Tests).
    - **No pack:** the grill's own items and cited research only (rule 11); a project pack drafted for the person to confirm; an `agk-proposal` offered.
    - **Several domains or jurisdictions:** all compose (rule 4); a conflict goes to the person with both sources; the stricter holds until decided.
    - **The customer's rule against the pack:** the customer's rule wins for business practice; a conflict with a regulation goes to the person; recorded either way as a `Decision:`.
    - **A stale regulation:** an `as_of` older than `stale_months` is shown with its date and `(verify)`; `check_domains.py` warns, never fails.
    - **A pilot:** SPEC-0073's `light` grill, but every regulated pack's critical items are still asked, and a deferral is a `Decision:` with its reason.
    - **No person confirms in time:** past `[flow] owner_wait_minutes` the item stays a `D-<n>` open point, held at the design when critical (rule 9).
    - **A business rule changes:** the steward lists the specs, briefs, tests and code that name that `BR-<n>` and asks which to change; nothing changes silently.
    - **Confidential domain knowledge:** stays in `docs/domain/` and the project pack; never in a kit pack, an `agk-proposal` or outbound text (the outbound guard checks).
    - **Sensitive data classes:** personal, health or payment data in `docs/domain/data.md` adds the class's detectors to the data guard (SPEC-0042) and forbids real records in fixtures and logs.
    - **An adopted codebase:** rules inferred from code are `inferred` until the person confirms them, in batches of at most five.
    - **The person's own words:** a term not in the glossary is asked once and added as a synonym; the steward then uses the person's term.
    - **Grill budget:** domain items share it; a regulated pack's critical items are asked even when it is spent, and the grill says so.
    - **Records before this lands:** read as today; no `Rules:` line is required of a brief whose project has no `docs/domain/`.
    - **The kit itself:** has no domain; `[domain]` is read only in a project.
15. **Solo, team, personal, execution and layouts.**
    - **Solo:** plain words with no kit term (SPEC-0062 rule 5); the one person confirms with a plain yes.
    - **Team:** rule 9's confirmations; domain items route as area `product` by SPEC-0073 rule 12 and amendment 238.
    - **Personal mode** (a sidecar with `forge = "files"`): the packs and the domain model are in the sidecar's files; questions are printed; nothing is posted.
    - **Execution** (`hosted`, `hybrid`, `pool`, `local`): `check_domains.py` and the `Rules:` check are file-only inside the existing `playbooks` and brief checks; local mode runs them in `prepush.py` and `make -f agk.mk agk-governance`; no workflow, `agk.mk` recipe or `governance_map.CHECKS` change.
    - **Layouts:** `config/domains/` (the shipped packs), `config/domain-signals.toml` and `check_domains.py` join `kit/manifest.json` `verbatim` and `kit/hashes.json` (and `tests/kit_dev/test_kit_manifest.py`) for the installed layout and ship in the plugin snapshot for the clean layout (SPEC-0071); the project's own pack is read first, in `engines.playbook_path`'s order.

17. **Measured goals.** In a recorded planning conversation for an invented payments product (`example.com`): the domain is recognised with one yes; the pack's critical items are asked within the grill's budget; the steward proposes the requirements the pack names that the person did not mention, each with its source; the stack ADR shows each option's domain fit; every `BR-<n>` has a source and an owner; a change that breaks a named rule gets a fix list naming it; no domain claim in a steward reply lacks a source; no customer text appears in a kit pack or outbound text. Governance grows by less than 2 seconds on the kit, measured with `kit_root.py governance` before and after.
