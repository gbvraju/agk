# SPEC-0035: A shadow reviewer reviews the same heads as the conductor, builders mark every finding at merge, and AGK reports the conductor's catch rate from the marks

- Status: Approved
- Owner: reviewer group (a team from config/reviewers.yaml)
- Requirements: REQ-042

Status is `Draft` or `Approved`. An Approved spec is owned by its reviewer group; agents propose patches.
Written by the intake of one sentence (SPEC-0012): "A second reviewer reviews the same pull request heads as the conductor without seeing its verdicts, every finding of either is marked right or wrong at merge, and AGK reports the conductor's catch rate from those marks instead of anyone stating one." It is generic and project-agnostic: it names no project, no runtime and no launcher, and a GitHub repository, a shell, `GET` and comment `POST`s are all it needs. The brief of issue #207 gives eight answers; they are the conductor's proposals, and the maintainer agreed on 2026-10-25 only to start this intake. The eight points that needed a ruling were put as questions `Q-S0035-1` to `Q-S0035-8`, and the maintainer ruled on all of them on 2026-10-25 (posted on the pull request of this intake, recorded in `docs/runs/decisions-log.md`); the rules below apply the answers and the rulings. The conductor role file says that only the shadow review measures a catch rate (`.claude/agents/conductor.md`), and no shadow review exists yet; the cases that made it necessary are in the brief (a wrong cause named for a red main branch, a hashes test that would fail later builds, a spec called fully built when one sentence was not), each found by a builder or a person and not counted. Negative cases: a mark by the reviewer of its own finding is refused, a head without marks is `unmarked` and never zero, no rate is printed without a right finding, and nothing in the shadow review merges, starts or stops a session.

## Rules

1. **The shadow reviewer.** A session runs the role file `.claude/agents/shadow.md`, a file of its own (the conductor's file is not reused, because the conductor posts verdicts), with its own guard line in `.claude/hooks/conductor_guard.py` that allows one comment `POST` on the one open issue labelled `agk-shadow` and no other write. It posts no verdict on a pull request. For every head the conductor has reviewed or will review, each fix list and each re-push and not only the final head, it posts one comment on the one open issue labelled `agk-shadow`, whose first line is `Shadow: #<pr> <sha> cleared|fix list|rejected`, followed by numbered findings `S1.`, `S2.` with the path and the line, in the shape of a fix list. It edits no file, never merges and starts, stops and reviews no session.
2. **Blind.** Before it posts, the shadow reads the pull request, its checks and its diff and never the verdict comments of the conductor on that pull request. The role file says so. While both reviewers post from one account, no check can tell them apart; the rule is the role file's, as for the conductor (SPEC-0032 rule 6), and it is a residual below. For the first sample of ten the blindness of the role file is enough; the sample does not wait for separate accounts.
3. **The marks.** After a pull request merges, each numbered finding of the conductor's verdicts and of the shadow's comments for that pull request is marked once, in one comment on the pull request whose first line is `Marks:`, followed by one line per finding: `<C|S><sha7>-<n> right|wrong|both`, where `C` is a finding of the conductor, `S` a finding of the shadow, `<sha7>` the first seven characters of the head the finding was made on and `<n>` its number in that comment, and `both` means the other reviewer raised the same finding and it is right. For the ten pull requests of the sample the maintainer writes the marks, or at least confirms every `wrong` mark. After the sample the builder of the pull request writes the marks and a person may change any mark. A pull request whose builder has ended before the merge is marked by the person who merges it, at merge. The conductor and the shadow never mark their own findings, and where the accounts differ a mark by the author of the finding is refused.
4. **The count.** `scripts/agk/shadow_count.py` reads saved copies of the comments of the pull requests, the issue and the verdicts, and no network. It prints, per ISO week of the merge and in total: the heads reviewed by both, the findings right for the conductor only, for the shadow only and for both, the wrong findings of each, and one unweighted catch rate (a weighting by the kind of finding is a later decision, if the sample shows the need), which is the right conductor findings (those marked `right` or `both`) divided by the right conductor findings plus the right shadow-only findings. A head with no `Marks:` comment is counted as `unmarked`, never as zero findings, and with no right finding the rate is printed as `none`, never as 0. A `Shadow:` comment that matches no verdict head is `unpaired`, and a mark line that names a finding found in no verdict or `Shadow:` comment of that pull request is refused naming the rule and never the line.
5. **The report.** The weekly report gains the catch-rate line and the counts of rule 4 next to the conductor's verdicts. This raises the report schema (it stands at 6 on 2026-10-25), and build (c) is held until the portal reader of the new number is merged, with a line `Readers: <issue or pull request of each known reader>` in its body, as the raises of schemas 4 to 6 did.
6. **The sample.** The shadow reviews the next ten builder pull requests merged after its build, and `docs/runs/shadow-review-1.md` records the counts and the rate of those ten. The catch rate is the only number any launch text may state about the conductor; launch text is the README, the guides and `docs/runs/`, and a test checks the README and the guides.
7. **Where each comment lives.** On the pull request: `Marks:`. On the one open issue labelled `agk-shadow`: `Shadow:`. The session rules (`config/session-rules.md`) say so, with the shapes of rules 1 and 3.
8. **No network, no names.** The count and the report read files and saved copies only. Messages name the rule, never a finding, a sentence or a value. Nothing in the files names a project.
9. **How it changes.** Intake first (REQ-042, this spec and ADR-0129, approved by the maintainer's `Approve:` line, SPEC-0032 rule 5), then builds of about 600 changed lines each: (a) the shadow role file with its guard line, the `agk-shadow` issue and the `Shadow:` and `Marks:` shapes in the session rules; (b) `shadow_count.py` and its tests; (c) the report line with its schema raise, after the portal reader.
10. **Tests.** Saved comments in fixtures, no network: a `Shadow:` comment parsed; a `Marks:` comment with right, wrong and both; an unmarked head; the rate from a fixture week; a conductor finding marked by the conductor refused; the texts of the role file and the session rules.

## Tests

- Rule 1: the `Shadow:` first line and the `S1.` findings are parsed, a comment of another shape is ignored, and a `Shadow:` comment that matches no verdict is unpaired; REQ-042 AC1.
- Rules 1 and 2: the text of the role file says the shadow posts no verdict, reads no verdict comment of the conductor before posting, and never merges or starts a session, and the guard allows one comment `POST` on the `agk-shadow` issue and refuses any other write; REQ-042 AC2.
- Rule 3: a `Marks:` comment with a right, a wrong and a both line counts each finding once, and a line that names an unknown finding is refused naming the rule; REQ-042 AC3.
- Rules 3 and 4: a mark by the author of the finding it marks is refused where the accounts differ, and a head without marks is `unmarked` and never zero findings; REQ-042 AC4.
- Rule 4: the counts and the rate of a fixture week and of the total, and `none` for a week without a right finding; REQ-042 AC5.
- Rule 5: the weekly report of a fixture shows the line and the counts, and a schema raise without a `Readers:` line and its merged reader is held; REQ-042 AC6.
- Rule 6: the sample record states the counts and the rate of the ten, and a test finds no other number about the conductor in the README and the guides; REQ-042 AC7.
- Rules 7 and 8: the session rules give the shapes and where each comment lives, and nothing in the files starts, stops or reviews a session or merges; REQ-042 AC8.

## Proof

REQ-042 AC1 and AC2: *a second reviewer sees the same heads and does not see the conductor's verdicts.* Rules 1 and 2. REQ-042 AC3 and AC4: *every finding is marked once, by someone who did not raise it, and a missing mark is not a zero.* Rules 3 and 4. REQ-042 AC5: *the catch rate is computed from the marks, with the formula in the file and no rate without a right finding.* Rule 4. REQ-042 AC6: *the weekly report carries it.* Rule 5. REQ-042 AC7: *a sample of ten is recorded and the rate is the only figure stated about the conductor.* Rule 6. REQ-042 AC8: *the comments have one place each and nothing in the review controls a session.* Rules 7 and 8.

## Residual

- 2026-10-25: while the conductor and the shadow post from one account, a check cannot tell their comments apart and cannot refuse a mark by the author of a finding; the blindness of rule 2 rests on the role file and the first sample of ten does not wait for separate accounts (ruled on Q-S0035-2) (due 2026-12-31).
- 2026-10-25: the catch rate counts only findings that a builder or a person marked right; a defect that neither reviewer found is not in it, so the rate is a measure of what the pair finds and not of all defects (due 2026-12-31).
- 2026-10-25: no command yet posts a `Shadow:` or a `Marks:` comment; sessions post them with their runtime's tools until a build adds the commands (due 2026-12-31).

## Open questions

All ruled by the maintainer on 2026-10-25 (the rulings posted on the pull request of this intake and recorded in `docs/runs/decisions-log.md`); none is open.

- Q-S0035-1: a second role file or the conductor's? Ruled: a second role file, `.claude/agents/shadow.md`, with its own guard line allowing one comment `POST` on the open `agk-shadow` issue only (rule 1).
- Q-S0035-2: is blindness by the role file enough? Ruled: yes for the first sample of ten, recorded as a residual; the sample does not wait for separate accounts (rule 2).
- Q-S0035-3: who marks the sample? Ruled: the maintainer writes the marks, or at least confirms every `wrong` mark; after the sample the builder marks and a person may change any mark (rule 3).
- Q-S0035-4: who marks when the builder has ended? Ruled: the person who merges the pull request, at merge; an unmarked head is `unmarked`, never zero (rule 3).
- Q-S0035-5: weighted or not? Ruled: one unweighted catch rate for the first sample; weighting is a later decision (rule 4).
- Q-S0035-6: which heads? Ruled: every head the conductor gives a verdict on (rule 1).
- Q-S0035-7: what is launch text? Ruled: the README, the guides and `docs/runs/`; a test checks the README and the guides (rule 6).
- Q-S0035-8: is the schema raise held for the reader? Ruled: yes; build (c) is held until the portal reader of that schema is merged, with a `Readers:` line in its body (rule 5).
