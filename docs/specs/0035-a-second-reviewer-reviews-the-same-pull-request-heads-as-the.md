# SPEC-0035: A shadow reviewer reviews the same heads as the conductor, builders mark every finding at merge, and AGK reports the conductor's catch rate from the marks

- Status: Draft
- Owner: reviewer group (a team from config/reviewers.yaml)
- Requirements: REQ-042

Status is `Draft` or `Approved`. An Approved spec is owned by its reviewer group; agents propose patches.
Written by the intake of one sentence (SPEC-0012): "A second reviewer reviews the same pull request heads as the conductor without seeing its verdicts, every finding of either is marked right or wrong at merge, and AGK reports the conductor's catch rate from those marks instead of anyone stating one." It is generic and project-agnostic: it names no project, no runtime and no launcher, and a GitHub repository, a shell, `GET` and comment `POST`s are all it needs. The brief of issue #207 gives eight answers; they are the conductor's proposals, and the maintainer agreed on 2026-10-25 only to start this intake, so the rules below follow them as written and every point that needs the maintainer's ruling is a question under `## Open questions`, not a decision. The conductor role file says that only the shadow review measures a catch rate (`.claude/agents/conductor.md`), and no shadow review exists yet; the cases that made it necessary are in the brief (a wrong cause named for a red main branch, a hashes test that would fail later builds, a spec called fully built when one sentence was not), each found by a builder or a person and not counted. Negative cases: a mark by the reviewer of its own finding is refused, a head without marks is `unmarked` and never zero, no rate is printed without a right finding, and nothing in the shadow review merges, starts or stops a session.

## Rules

1. **The shadow reviewer.** A session runs a role file for the shadow reviewer, which is the conductor's role file (`.claude/agents/conductor.md`) with one difference: it posts no verdict on a pull request. For a head the conductor has reviewed or will review it posts one comment on the one open issue labelled `agk-shadow`, whose first line is `Shadow: #<pr> <sha> cleared|fix list|rejected`, followed by numbered findings `S1.`, `S2.` with the path and the line, in the shape of a fix list. It edits no file, never merges and starts, stops and reviews no session.
2. **Blind.** Before it posts, the shadow reads the pull request, its checks and its diff and never the verdict comments of the conductor on that pull request. The role file says so. While both reviewers post from one account, no check can tell them apart; the rule is the role file's and its residual is SPEC-0032 rule 6.
3. **The marks.** After a pull request merges, each numbered finding of the conductor's verdicts and of the shadow's comments for that pull request is marked once, in one comment on the pull request whose first line is `Marks:`, followed by one line per finding: `<C|S><sha7>-<n> right|wrong|both`, where `C` is a finding of the conductor, `S` a finding of the shadow, `<sha7>` the first seven characters of the head the finding was made on and `<n>` its number in that comment, and `both` means the other reviewer raised the same finding and it is right. The builder of the pull request writes the marks and a person may change them. The conductor and the shadow never mark their own findings, and where the accounts differ a mark by the author of the finding is refused.
4. **The count.** `scripts/agk/shadow_count.py` reads saved copies of the comments of the pull requests, the issue and the verdicts, and no network. It prints, per ISO week of the merge and in total: the heads reviewed by both, the findings right for the conductor only, for the shadow only and for both, the wrong findings of each, and the catch rate, which is the right conductor findings (those marked `right` or `both`) divided by the right conductor findings plus the right shadow-only findings. A head with no `Marks:` comment is counted as `unmarked`, never as zero findings, and with no right finding the rate is printed as `none`, never as 0. A `Shadow:` comment that matches no verdict head is `unpaired`, and a mark line that names a finding found in no verdict or `Shadow:` comment of that pull request is refused naming the rule and never the line.
5. **The report.** The weekly report gains the catch-rate line and the counts of rule 4 next to the conductor's verdicts. This raises the report schema, and the raise waits for the portal reader, as the raises of schemas 4 to 6 did.
6. **The sample.** The shadow reviews the next ten builder pull requests merged after its build, and `docs/runs/shadow-review-1.md` records the counts and the rate of those ten. The catch rate is the only number any launch text may state about the conductor.
7. **Where each comment lives.** On the pull request: `Marks:`. On the one open issue labelled `agk-shadow`: `Shadow:`. The session rules (`config/session-rules.md`) say so, with the shapes of rules 1 and 3.
8. **No network, no names.** The count and the report read files and saved copies only. Messages name the rule, never a finding, a sentence or a value. Nothing in the files names a project.
9. **How it changes.** Intake first (REQ-042, this spec and ADR-0129, approved by the maintainer's `Approve:` line, SPEC-0032 rule 5), then builds of about 600 changed lines each: (a) the shadow role file, the `agk-shadow` issue and the `Shadow:` and `Marks:` shapes in the session rules; (b) `shadow_count.py` and its tests; (c) the report line with its schema raise, after the portal reader.
10. **Tests.** Saved comments in fixtures, no network: a `Shadow:` comment parsed; a `Marks:` comment with right, wrong and both; an unmarked head; the rate from a fixture week; a conductor finding marked by the conductor refused; the texts of the role file and the session rules.

## Tests

- Rule 1: the `Shadow:` first line and the `S1.` findings are parsed, a comment of another shape is ignored, and a `Shadow:` comment that matches no verdict is unpaired; REQ-042 AC1.
- Rules 1 and 2: the text of the role file says the shadow posts no verdict, reads no verdict comment of the conductor before posting, and never merges or starts a session; REQ-042 AC2.
- Rule 3: a `Marks:` comment with a right, a wrong and a both line counts each finding once, and a line that names an unknown finding is refused naming the rule; REQ-042 AC3.
- Rules 3 and 4: a mark by the author of the finding it marks is refused where the accounts differ, and a head without marks is `unmarked` and never zero findings; REQ-042 AC4.
- Rule 4: the counts and the rate of a fixture week and of the total, and `none` for a week without a right finding; REQ-042 AC5.
- Rule 5: the weekly report of a fixture shows the line and the counts, and a schema raise without its reader is held; REQ-042 AC6.
- Rule 6: the sample record states the counts and the rate of the ten, and no guide or launch text states another number about the conductor; REQ-042 AC7.
- Rules 7 and 8: the session rules give the shapes and where each comment lives, and nothing in the files starts, stops or reviews a session or merges; REQ-042 AC8.

## Proof

REQ-042 AC3 and AC4: *every finding is marked once, by someone who did not raise it, and a missing mark is not a zero.* Rules 3 and 4. REQ-042 AC5: *the catch rate is computed from the marks, with the formula in the file and no rate without a right finding.* Rule 4. REQ-042 AC6: *the weekly report carries it.* Rule 5. REQ-042 AC8: *the comments have one place each and nothing in the review controls a session.* Rules 7 and 8.

## Open questions

- Q-S0035-1: Rule 1 reads "the conductor's role file with one difference". Is the shadow a second role file (`.claude/agents/shadow.md` with its own guard line in `conductor_guard.py`, which today allows one comment `POST` and may need to allow the `agk-shadow` issue), or the same file started with a different instruction? The criteria assume a second role file, because the conductor's own text says that a verdict is its only `POST`.
- Q-S0035-2: Rule 2 cannot be enforced on one account, and a reviewer can always read the verdicts with `gh api GET`. Is blindness by the role file's rule enough for the sample of ten to count, or must the sample wait until the shadow posts from a separate account?
- Q-S0035-3: Rule 3 has the builder of a pull request mark the findings made on its own work, and a `wrong` mark favours the builder. Is that right for the sample, or should a person mark at least the findings of the pull requests in the sample? The criteria assume the builder marks and a person may change a mark.
- Q-S0035-4: A builder may end before its pull request merges (SPEC-0034: a builder's last comment is a `Report:`, a `released:` or a `Request:`). Who writes the marks of a pull request whose builder has ended, and when: before the builder's `Request:`, or the person at merge? The criteria assume the marks are written after the merge, before the `Request:`, and an unmarked head counts as `unmarked`.
- Q-S0035-5: Rule 4 sets the catch rate as right conductor findings over right conductor findings plus right shadow-only findings. It counts a finding by its mark and not by its severity, and it leaves out defects that neither reviewer found. Is that the number, or should findings be weighted (for instance a finding that would have failed CI later against a style nit)? The criteria assume one unweighted number, because the brief names one.
- Q-S0035-6: The shadow reviews "a head the conductor has reviewed or will review". Does it review every head of a pull request (each fix list and each re-push) or the head of the final verdict only? The criteria assume every head the conductor gives a verdict on, as `<sha7>` in the finding ids implies.
- Q-S0035-7: Rule 6 limits launch text to the catch rate as the only number about the conductor. Which text does that cover (the guide, the README, `docs/runs/`), and is a check wanted, or is the rule a convention? The criteria assume the check is a test of the guides and the README, not of every file.
- Q-S0035-8: Rule 5 raises the report schema once more. The number is not fixed here (the schemas stand at 5 on 2026-10-25). Is build (c) held until the portal reader accepts the next number, as the learning on #176 says (a `Readers:` line in the pull request body)? The criteria assume yes.
