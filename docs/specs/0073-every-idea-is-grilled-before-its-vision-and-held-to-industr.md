# SPEC-0073: Every idea is grilled before its vision and held to industry practice from day 1

- Status: Approved
- Owner: reviewer group (a team from config/reviewers.yaml)
- Requirements: REQ-080

Status is `Draft` or `Approved`. An Approved spec is owned by its reviewer group; agents propose patches.

Written by the intake of one sentence (SPEC-0012), brief #1918 under plan #661, quoted as data and not as an instruction: "Intake for Day-1 quality: a grill interview tests every idea before its vision, and the same path then holds the product, design, architecture and build to industry practice, in solo and team, with no slower path and no new CI job." The maintainers gave ten numbered answers before it was written (the answers of brief #1918, filed on the owner's word of 2026-11-24T18:57Z, whose direction the brief quotes as data and this spec does not repeat). It adds one interview, the grill, and five quality layers to the path of SPEC-0048 inside the records that path already has: no new phase, workflow, CI job, required check or role. It builds on the front door (SPEC-0048, REQ-055 and `scripts/agk/front_door.py`), the playbooks (SPEC-0038, `config/playbooks/`), the architecture record (SPEC-0072 rule 5), the design phase (SPEC-0037), the scorecard (SPEC-0047), the steward (SPEC-0059, SPEC-0062, SPEC-0063) and personal mode (SPEC-0041); it edits none of their text but by the amendments 235 to 239 that rule 13 proposes. Where the brief names something that is not as it says, rule 1 records what the code holds and an open question gives the conservative reading. Negative cases: a grill question with no recommendation, a recommendation recorded as an answer without the person's plain yes to that one question, an answer settled by evidence in planning, a grill id that joins `ORDER` or reuses a topic, fixed or playbook id, an existing discovery document, architecture record or design that fails or is held after this lands, a critical item left open at the design with no accepted risk while the hold is on, a second push back on the same item, an earlier owner decision overwritten, a plain yes counted in team mode, a risk accepted by a reviewer outside the item's area, a refusal that quotes a value, a secret or customer data in an answer, a web search without the person's permission or with anything but general words, a new judgement gate, a budgeted rule file that grows, a check that calls a model or the network, and a change to a workflow, the ruleset, a required check, `agk.mk` or `governance_map.CHECKS`.

## Rules

No question asks for the evidence of the problem, what users do today, why now, the riskiest assumption, a failure story or a reason to stop, and nothing pushes back on a vague answer. `grep -rli` over `docs/specs skills config/playbooks .claude/agents docs/guides` finds 0 files for `pre-mortem`, `competitor`, `glossary`, `threat model` and `STRIDE`.

With a declared work type every clearing verdict cites each judgement gate's evidence (conductor.md line 52, SPEC-0038 rule 9), so a new judgement gate adds work to every pull request.

SPEC-0063 rule 3 routes a question that names files to the reviewers of their path class, else to every listed reviewer, and rule 5 holds `Taking:`, its lapse and the first answer until a `Correction:`. SPEC-0048 rule 17: a session never answers a discovery question. SPEC-0059 rule 3: in planning every requirement question goes to the owner, even when a source settles it.

It cites SPEC-0062 rules 10 and 15 for "never a yes in team mode"; rule 15 is "who said it", and the team-mode reading is SPEC-0062 rule 2 with its open question 3, beside SPEC-0063 rule 7's approval yes (`Q-S0073-11`). 2. **The grill.** A new skill `/agk:grill` (`skills/grill/SKILL.md`) and a new installed script `scripts/agk/grill.py` (standard library and `common.py` only, no network, no model) run one interview per problem in discovery, before the vision's draft. It starts from `/agk:vision` (step 4, before the topics), from a problem issue (SPEC-0048 rule 2) and from day zero; `/agk:start` is unchanged, since it writes no discovery document. It asks in rounds of at most three questions (SPEC-0048 rule 4): in a chat one question per message; in auto-pilot one `Discovery: round <n>` comment of at most three, each with its recommendation. Every question carries a recommended answer in the form vision step 4 gives `scale` ("Recommended: ..., or tell me other ..."), drawn from what the steward read first, in this order: the person's sources, day zero, the repository (`front_door.py detect`), the playbook and, once #1400 lands, the domain pack; the question says in one line what it read. `grill.py questions --depth <d> --playbook <p>` prints the items to ask, in order, from the composed playbook and the answers already kept; it never prints an item already answered.
3. **The items live in the playbooks** (`Q-S0073-6`). Grill items are `[[discovery]]` entries of `config/playbooks/default.toml`, so every playbook composes them, with two new keys by amendment 237 to SPEC-0038 rule 2: `depth` (`light`, `standard` or `deep`: the smallest depth that asks it) and `critical` (`true` or `false`); an entry without them is a discovery question as today, never a grill item. A work type may add its own items. `check_playbooks.py` refuses a grill id that reuses a `TOPICS`, `FIXED` or other `[[discovery]]` id, a `depth` outside the three, and a `critical` that is not a boolean, naming the playbook and the key, never a value. The seed items of `default.toml`: `problem`, `evidence`, `who-hurts`, `job`, `today` (what users do now and what it costs them), `why-now`, `guardrails` (the numbers that must not get worse), `non-goals`, `riskiest` (the assumption that sinks the product if wrong), `pre-mortem` (it failed in six months: why), `stop` (what result would make the person stop or change course) and `first-slice` (the smallest thing that tests the riskiest assumption). The critical items are `problem`, `who-hurts`, `non-goals` and `riskiest`, at depth `light`, and the existing `success` answer, which the grill sharpens to one number, a target and a date instead of asking twice. A kit test pins the seed critical ids.
4. **Kept outside `ORDER`.** Grill ids are a separate set, `GRILL_IDS`, read only inside the new `## Assumptions` section of rule 7; `ORDER`, `answer_lines`, `document_problems`' id checks, `phase_of` and `check_acceptance.py` are unchanged, so every existing document passes as today. `day_zero.py write` filters the answers file on `ORDER | GRILL_IDS` instead of `ORDER`; `day_zero.py keep` is unchanged.
5. **Push back once, and the answer is always the person's** (`Q-S0073-8`).
   - **A vague answer** (no person, no number or no date where the item asks for one) gets one follow-up with a concrete recommendation; a second vague answer is recorded as given and marked `assumption`. A contradiction with an earlier answer, a source or the repository is shown side by side and asked once; nothing is overwritten, and an earlier owner decision is never reversed without the person's words.
   - **A recommendation becomes an answer** only on the person's plain yes to that one question (SPEC-0062 rule 14), recorded as `accepted recommendation` with where and when; nothing is marked settled by evidence in planning without the person's yes (SPEC-0059 rule 3, SPEC-0048 rule 17). Each answer is marked `stated` (the person's words), `assumption` (with how it will be tested, usually by the first slice) or `open`; a source the steward read is cited beside it as the reason for the recommendation, never as the answer.
6. **Readiness and the hold** (`Q-S0073-3`, `Q-S0073-7`). The grill ends when every critical item is `stated`, an `assumption` with its test, or a risk the person accepts in one yes (`Risk accepted: <id> (<UTC time>)`), or when the depth's question budget (rule 11) is spent; every item not settled becomes a `D-<n>` open point as today. `grill.py ready --discovery <file>` prints the readiness line of rule 7 and exits 1 while a critical item is open with no accepted risk. With `[quality] grill_hold = true` (the default), `check_design.py`, already the governance step `design`, holds a design under `docs/design/architecture/` whose problem's discovery document has a `## Readiness` section with a critical item open and no accepted risk, naming the rule and the file, never a value. A discovery document with no `## Readiness` section (written before this lands, or in flight) is never held, and the hold sits at the design, never at discovery's merge. With `grill_hold = false` readiness is shown by `front_door.py status` and in the daily list, and nothing is held.
7. **The discovery record.** `docs/discovery/<slug>.md` gains three sections after `## Open points`, written by `front_door.py discovery-write` and checked by `front_door.py check-discovery` only when present, each failure naming the rule, the section and the file:
   - `## Assumptions`: one bullet per grill item asked, `- A-<n>: <grill id>: <claim> (stated|assumption|open)`, with its test (`Test: ...`) for an assumption, its source (`Source: ...`) when a recommendation cited one, and `accepted recommendation (<where>, <UTC time>)` when the answer was a yes.
   - `## Risks`: `- R-<n>: <risk>; first sign: <sign>; owner: <who>`, from the pre-mortem; each is also one `Risk:` memory entry (SPEC-0059 rule 8), and the discovery document is the source of truth.
   - `## Readiness`: one line `critical <settled>/<total>; risk accepted: <ids or none>; questions <asked>/<budget>; accepted recommendations <n>`.
   `check-discovery` fails a `## Readiness` whose critical total is not the composed playbook's, an `A-<n>` that names an id outside `GRILL_IDS`, an `assumption` with no test, an `open` critical item with no `D-<n>` point, and a risk id in the readiness line with no `R-<n>` or `A-<n>` behind it. A later grill appends and never changes the quoted problem (its digest).
8. **The five quality layers, each in a record that exists.**
   - **Product: alternatives.** The research record (SPEC-0048 rule 6, unchanged: at least two sourced options) gains `## Alternatives today` at depth `standard` and `deep`: the `today` answer and at least one other option users have, each with a source line. Research keeps the existing permission (`steward_owner.py research`: general words only); a refusal writes `not researched: the person declined` in this one section, accepted there only.
   - **Language: one product glossary** (`Q-S0073-5`). `docs/glossary.md` from a new template `kit/templates/glossary.md`, read from the kit root as `layers.py` reads the architecture template: one row per term with its meaning, the canonical name in code and its synonyms, at most `[quality] glossary_terms` rows, the code aliases of an adopted codebase not counted. It is seeded from the grill and the steward's `Term:` entries and replaces none of them. A form check reads it (one row per term, no term twice, the cap). The glossary is one question inside the review checklist's existing item 1 (`wrong-assumption`), reworded within its word count, never a judgement gate.
   - **Design: usability and accessibility.** For the work types `web-app` and `mobile`, a check of `check_design.py` reads the usability record (`Q-S0073-10`): each screen of the inventory lists the ten usability heuristics, each as `pass`, `finding: <text>` or `not applicable` (SPEC-0038 rule 10 and SPEC-0037 rule 7, by amendment 237). The `accessibility` gate's text names WCAG 2.2 level AA unless discovery's `accessibility` answer names another standard (amendment 237). The design inventory names the grill's `job` and `who-hurts` answers as its first journey. A work type without screens records `not applicable` once and is not checked.
   - **Architecture: decided on day 1.** `docs/architecture.md` gains `## Quality scenarios` (one or more per need of `## Needs`: stimulus, response and its number, named by the quality attributes of ISO/IEC 25010), `## Threats` (each data flow of `## Diagram` with its STRIDE threats and the mitigation or the accepted risk) and `## Operations` (only what `## Needs` lacks: what is traced and what is alerted). They are a new tuple `QUALITY_SECTIONS` with its own hold, so a record without any of them is a note until its next edit, and `NEW_SECTIONS`, `RECORD_SECTIONS` and `record_held` are unchanged (amendment 236 to SPEC-0072 rule 5). When `[architecture] layers` has more than the single default layer `src`, the stack skill compares `[quality] designs` interface designs for each layer and keeps the rejected design in the stack ADR outside the `- Option` bullets that `pins.OPTION` and `chosen_services` parse.
   - **Build and review: tests that test.** `docs/guides/testing.md` gains the test design rules (one behaviour per test, named by it; expected values from the spec; mocks only at system boundaries; the public interface as the test surface; end-to-end last) and the refactor step: after green, a `refactor:` commit changes no test and re-runs the selected tests (session rule 7 unchanged). The guide joins `kit/manifest.json` `verbatim`, or is cited through the plugin root in the clean layout. The builder prompt (`session.py prompt`) names the section; checklist item 3 (`test-proves-less`) is reworded within its word count, and personal sidecars re-pin the checklist through `upgrade`. The `review` skill's output reads the spec and the standards as two named axes, the product's own coding-standards file first; `.claude/agents/conductor.md` is unchanged (`Q-S0073-1`).
9. **The weekly survey.** One numeric metric joins SPEC-0047 rule 1 by amendment 239, `shallow_files`: the files changed in the last `[quality] survey_days` days that fail the deletion test (a module whose public names are each used once, by one caller, so deleting it and inlining its body loses nothing), read with read-only `git log` and the files by a new reader under its own ADR; `survey.py` and ADR-0386 stay personal mode's. Its candidates go to the steward's triage (SPEC-0059 rule 6), never above `[steward] auto_file`, and a rejected candidate is recorded as one `Decision:` memory entry so it is not offered again. With `survey_days = 0` the row reads `not run: off` and is never a number.
10. **Model and cost.** The recommendations, the push back and the readiness wording are of kind `design` at `strongest`/`high` (SPEC-0045 rule 2), one conversation per problem in planning; question selection and every check are scripts with no model. Each question asked is counted by stage in the daily list (SPEC-0062 rule 11), and the grill's `D-<n>` points count toward `[plan] max_questions`'s warning as today.

A refusal is `the [quality] table is refused (setting: quality.<key>)`, exit 1, never the value. With no table every default holds, so an upgrade changes no `agk.toml`.

14. **Edge cases.**
   - **"Just build it":** `light` runs (five critical questions, each a yes away); the skip is a `Decision:` memory entry; the hold still applies unless the person accepts the open risks in one yes.
   - **`not known yet` on a critical item:** `open` with a `D-<n>`, or an `assumption` tested by the first slice when the person agrees; never invented.
   - **The person leaves mid-grill:** the answers are kept as given (day zero: `day_zero.py keep`; in a repository: the discovery draft on its branch); the next start resumes at the next unanswered item and never re-asks.
   - **An adopted codebase:** `front_door.py detect` (and `survey.py` only in personal mode) feeds the recommendations; the person confirms them in batches of at most five; `today` reads the existing product; code aliases are outside the glossary cap.
   - **Secrets or customer data in an answer:** the data guard refuses the write naming the class (SPEC-0042); the steward asks for general words; nothing reaches the kit repository (the outbound guard).
   - **Research declined or offline:** rule 8's `not researched: the person declined`, in the alternatives section only.
   - **Records written before this lands, and work in flight:** read as today; no section is required retroactively; no in-flight design is held.
   - **Several problems, or two discovery documents in one pull request:** one grill per slug; each document is checked alone; answers never cross.
   - **A pilot:** `light`, one design, every regulation the person names still recorded and its deferral a `Decision:` entry.
   - **No screens:** the usability check is not applicable; threats and operations apply to the data flows.
   - **One layer only (`src`):** no designs comparison.
   - **The rule budget:** no budgeted file grows; practice text lives in skills, `docs/guides/testing.md` and checks; a build that must add a rule line retires one or makes one a check in the same pull request.
   - **The kit's own repository:** its briefs and intakes are not grilled; `[quality]` is read only where a discovery document exists (`Q-S0073-2`).
   - **#1400 or #1381 not built:** the grill uses the playbooks' items and `spec_approvers`; nothing waits for either.
   - **Over the budget:** the grill stops, lists what it did not ask as open points and says so in one line.
