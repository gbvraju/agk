- `smoke.py [--base REF] [--head REF] [--run]` chooses the smoke run after a merge from what changed: one kind of the new `[testing] smoke` list (`frontend` or `contract`) when every changed file outside documentation matches its `code`, nothing for documentation only, and the full smoke for anything else; it prints the choice and why, and `--run` runs it under the heavy-step lock. It is never a gate, and without the list nothing changes (brief #923, SPEC-0044 build (c), ADR-0270)
