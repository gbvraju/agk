- Packs: every kind of knowledge (playbooks, standards, stack defaults, stack and domain signals) is read in one order through the new resolver `packs.files`: the kit's built-in `agk-core`, then each pack of `[packs] use` in its listed order, then the project's own files; a pack's restated gate or standard holds only when the same or stronger, and a weakening is refused naming the pack and the key; a pack's signal or stack-default row replaces the kit's of the same name and stays a proposal; with no `[packs]` table every reader gives today's result (SPEC-0077 build (b), brief #2032, ADR-0839)
